## Yershat Sapazhanov

I build machine learning systems, and I teach. Associate Professor in Almaty,
running two BSc programmes in statistics and data science.

Most of my recent work is reinforcement learning applied to things that
actually run: controller tuning for nonlinear thermal plants with transport
delay and sensor noise, and risk-sensitive supervision at an open-pit gold
mine, where I am principal investigator on an industry-funded R&D contract.

### Live systems

- **[aqylzhan.kz](https://aqylzhan.kz)** — adaptive mathematics and logic
  trainer for grades 1–6 in Kazakh, Russian and English. An item bank of 23,841
  problems across 34 topics, with paired Elo ratings for learners and items
  updated on every answer, so difficulty is estimated from how children
  actually respond rather than assigned by hand.
- **[mockpanel.kz](https://mockpanel.kz)** — IELTS Academic and Cambridge
  preparation covering all four exam skills, with AI-generated writing feedback
  and a spaced-repetition vocabulary trainer.

### Published code and data

- **[10.5281/zenodo.23142893](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23142893)** — code,
  data and notebooks for a study comparing six PID tuning strategies on a
  nonlinear thermal plant: Ziegler–Nichols, Cohen–Coon, differential evolution,
  a Smith predictor, and reinforcement learning in offline and online form.
